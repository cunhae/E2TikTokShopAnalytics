# Privacy Policy

**E2TikTokShopAnalytics**

Last updated: November 2, 2026

E2TikTokShopAnalytics is an analytics application developed to monitor and analyze TikTok account activity and TikTok Shop sales performance.

## 1. Information We Collect

With user authorization, the application may access TikTok profile information, video metrics, live stream performance, engagement statistics, product performance, and TikTok Shop sales data, subject to the permissions granted.

## 2. How We Use Information

Collected information is used exclusively for analytics, performance monitoring, reporting, and improving TikTok Shop sales strategies.

## 3. Data Storage and Security

Data is stored in a controlled environment and protected through appropriate security measures. Access tokens and credentials are not intentionally made public.

## 4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information or share collected TikTok data with third parties for advertising purposes.

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

Data is retained only as long as necessary for the application's purposes. Users may request deletion of their stored information and revoke application access through their TikTok account settings.

## 6. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in application functionality or applicable requirements.

## 7. Contact

For privacy inquiries or data deletion requests, contact the application administrator through the contact information published in the application's GitHub repository.
